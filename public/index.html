Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About ProofDesk

Have questions? We have answers. If you need further help, feel free to contact us.

How does the client approval process work?
When an agency uploads a deliverable, clients receive an immediate notification in their portal. The client can inspect the asset, view past versions, and either click "Approve Deliverable" or "Request Revision" with detailed feedback. Every decision creates a permanent, tamper-proof record in the audit trail.
Do clients need to pay for seats?
No! All client reviewer seats are completely free across all plans. You can invite unlimited client stakeholders to review, comment, and sign off on deliverables without incurring additional monthly seat charges.
Can I customize invoices and receipts with my brand logo?
Yes! Workspace owners can upload an official company logo in the Settings panel. This logo automatically renders across your navigation header, customer invoices, and formatted printable receipt statements.
